Welcome

Welcome to Halo PC OG

Looking for the classic Halo PC experience? You've found it.

Halo PC OG is a community dedicated to preserving and growing the original Halo PC and Halo Custom Edition multiplayer scene. Whether you remember the days of KlanWars, Team Warfare League (TWL), ClanBase, UGS, or countless late-night custom games, our goal is to bring that experience back for both veteran and new players alike.

Join our Discord to participate in regular 8s and 10s, pickup games, community events, and competitive matches. We host classic Team Slayer, CTF, Assault, and other community favorites using dedicated servers and competitive settings inspired by the golden era of Halo PC.

Whether you're looking to relive the KlanWars and TWL days, reconnect with old Halo PC veterans, or discover why so many players still consider Halo PC one of the greatest multiplayer games ever made, Halo PC OG is your home.
